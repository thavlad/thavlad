Help Centre

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days. Express delivery in Lagos arrives the next working day.

How much is delivery?

Standard delivery is free on orders of ₦50,000 or more and ₦1,500 below that. Express delivery is ₦3,000.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay by card, by bank transfer, or with cash or POS on delivery.

How do I track my order?

Open My account to see your orders. Each order shows its tracking number.

What is your return policy?

Contact support within 7 days of delivery if an item is damaged or not as described.

Still need help?

Email support@shopng.ng and we'll get back to you within one working day.
